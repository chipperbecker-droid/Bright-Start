Policies

The short version of our key policies. The full text is in the 2026–2027 School Catalogue (PDF).

Non-discrimination

Bright Start Medical Training admits students without discrimination based on race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, or political affiliation. Applicants who need an accommodation may contact the school at 302-307-3939 or info@brightstart-training.com.

Refund policy

All money paid will be refunded if you ask within three days after signing the enrollment agreement, but before starting classes. Refunds are made within 30 days of withdrawal or termination and are based on your last day of actual attendance.

Delaware's refund policy allows the school to keep $100 of tuition and fees. The minimum refund of the remaining tuition is:

Portion of the course completedRefund of remaining tuition
0.01% to 4.9%80%
5% to 9.9%70%
10% to 14.9%60%
15% to 24.9%55%
25% to 49.9%30%
50% or moreThe school may keep all remaining tuition

Withdrawal policy

To withdraw, notify the Program Director in writing using the school's Withdrawal Form. Your official withdrawal date is your last day of actual attendance. We encourage you to meet with the Dean of Students first to talk through your options. Any refund is calculated under the refund policy above.

Attendance

  • You may miss no more than 2 classes in total, across classroom, lab and practical training.
  • Tell your instructor at least 2 hours before class if you will be absent.
  • Arriving more than 15 minutes late counts as a tardy; 3 tardies equal 1 absence.
  • All missed time must be made up. School closures for weather never count against you.

Student complaints

First, bring the concern to your instructor. If it is not resolved, contact the Director of Training. If you are still not satisfied, you may write to:
