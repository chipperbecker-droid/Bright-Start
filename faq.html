Questions people ask

Can't find your answer? Call or text 302-307-3939.

Do I need a high school diploma?

Yes, a high school diploma, GED or equivalent is required by Delaware for this program.

How long is the program?

76 hours over about 3 weeks, Monday to Friday, 8:30 AM to 3:00 PM (the last day ends at 1:00 PM).

What if I miss a day?

You can miss no more than 2 classes in total, and missed time must be made up. Three late arrivals count as one absence. If the school closes for weather, it never counts against you; we use the built-in make-up days.

How much does it cost?

$1,785 in total, including books, CPR, background check, uniform and supplies. See Tuition & Funding for the payment schedule.

Will I be state certified?

Delaware does not certify or register home health aides. You get a Certificate of Completion that Delaware home health agencies accept as proof of required training.

Do you help me find a job?

Yes. We offer resume and interview support, career guidance and job placement assistance.

How do I pass?

Score at least 75% on quizzes and exams (one retake each) and pass the final skills evaluation.

What should I wear?

Clean scrubs, closed-toe non-skid shoes, a visible name badge, hair tied back, no artificial nails.

Can I transfer previous training?

Possibly. Send your transcripts or certificates and the Program Director will review them.

How do I become a home health aide in Delaware?

Delaware requires home health aides at licensed agencies to complete at least 75 hours of training. Our 76-hour program in Claymont covers classroom, skills lab and supervised practical training. You finish with a Certificate of Completion you can show Delaware home health agencies as evidence of required training.

What do I need to enroll?

You must be at least 18 and have a high school diploma, GED or equivalent and a valid government-issued ID. You also complete a criminal background check through DELJIS and a drug screening.

When does the next HHA class start?

New classes start about once a month through December 2027. See the current list on the Home Health Aide page.

Where are classes held?

At the Claymont Community Center, 3301 Green Street, Claymont, DE 19703.

Is CPR included in the HHA program?

Yes. CPR training is included in the $1,785 program cost, along with your textbook, uniform and supplies.

How much do CPR and BLS classes cost?

$95 each. The BLS course and the CPR, AED & First Aid course each take 4 hours. See CPR & BLS classes.

How long are CPR and BLS cards valid?

2 years. Every course earns an American Red Cross certification card.

Do you offer CNA training?

Not yet. Our Certified Nursing Assistant program is coming soon. Call or text 302-307-3939 to ask about it.

Ready to start?

The application takes about 10 minutes on your phone.
