What happens next

  1. We review your application and documents.

  2. We contact you within 2 business days to schedule your DELJIS background check and drug screening.

  3. Once you are accepted, you sign the enrollment agreement and we confirm your start date.

Good to know

A criminal history does not automatically disqualify you. Results are reviewed case by case. Need an accommodation? Call 302-307-3939.

The $100 registration fee is non-refundable and is applied to the total program cost of $1,785. Read the School Catalogue before you sign.
