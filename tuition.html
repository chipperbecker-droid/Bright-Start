How payment works

  1. $100 registration fee when you enroll (non-refundable, counts toward your total)

  2. Half of the remaining balance on the first day of class

  3. The rest by the end of week 2

We accept cash, money orders, and major credit and debit cards. Need a payment plan? Talk to our Business Manager before class starts: 302-307-3939.

Refunds

If you cancel within 3 days of signing your enrollment agreement and before classes begin, you get all your money back. After that, refunds follow Delaware's refund schedule, explained in section 33 of the School Catalogue and on our Policies page.

Is your employer paying?

Choose "My employer" or "Workforce or job-training funding" when you apply and we will work out the paperwork with you. Questions? Call 302-307-3939.
