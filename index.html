Questions people ask

Do I need a high school diploma?

Yes, a high school diploma, GED or equivalent is required by Delaware for this program.

How long is the program?

76 hours over about 3 weeks, Monday to Friday, 8:30 AM to 3:00 PM (the last day ends at 1:00 PM).

How much does it cost?

$1,785 in total, including books, CPR, background check, uniform and supplies. See Tuition & Funding for the payment schedule.

Will I be state certified?

Delaware does not certify or register home health aides. You get a Certificate of Completion that Delaware home health agencies accept as proof of required training.

Do you help me find a job?

Yes. We offer resume and interview support, career guidance and job placement assistance.
