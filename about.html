About Bright Start

A small, career-focused healthcare school in Claymont, Delaware.

Our mission

Bright Start Medical Training prepares compassionate, skilled healthcare professionals committed to high-quality patient care. We provide accessible, career-focused training that serves as a launchpad for long-term success in healthcare.

What we believe

Healthcare begins with dignity, respect and compassion. Home health aides help patients live safely and with dignity in their own homes, and our program focuses on real-world skills, accountability and hands-on practice so you are ready to work.

Our classroom and skills lab

Classroom: 24 students per instructor. Skills lab and practical training: 8 students per instructor.

Photo: classroom
Photo: skills lab with hospital bed and lift

The skills lab is set up like a patient's home, with a hospital bed, mechanical lift, wheelchair, walker, commode, vital-signs equipment and the everyday items you will use on the job.
